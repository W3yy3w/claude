The residents

Four ways this ends badly for you

Each animal has its own enclosure, its own handler and its own rules. Learn them before you go in.

Ophiophagus hannah

King Cobra

The longest venomous snake on Earth. Up to 5 metres, and it eats other snakes. One bite carries enough neurotoxin to stop an elephant's breathing.

Threat: extremeLength: up to 5.5 m
Python reticulatus

Reticulated Python

No venom needed. It strikes, wraps and squeezes. Malaysia's giants regularly pass 6 metres.

Threat: highLength: up to 7 m
Varanus salvator

Water Monitor

Two metres of muscle, claws and bacteria-loaded teeth. Fast on land, faster in water, and it bites back.

Threat: highLength: up to 2.5 m
Crocodylus porosus

Saltwater Crocodile

The apex predator of Malaysian rivers and the largest living reptile. Feeding time is the show nobody films twice, because they drop the phone.

Threat: extremeLength: up to 6 m
